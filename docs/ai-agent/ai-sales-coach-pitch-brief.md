# AI Sales Coach Pitch Brief

## What It Does

AI Sales Coach helps sales teams practice before real pipeline is on the line. Reps run live
role-plays with AI buyers that stay in character across a full sales call, push back realistically,
and adapt to the rep's actual questions and pitch. Managers use it to create repeatable coaching
moments for cold calls, discovery, objection handling, demos, and closing practice without joining
every session. The realtime buyer never scores, grades, or coaches during the call; it only acts as
the buyer.

## Who Buys It

The strongest buyers are Heads of Sales, VPs of Sales, sales enablement leaders, and founder-led
SaaS teams with 5 to 75 reps. They usually have one or more of these pains: new reps ramp slowly,
managers do not have enough time for role-play, messaging is inconsistent, reps avoid practicing
hard objections, or the team wants safer practice before live prospect calls.

## Pricing

Use simple pilot-first pricing in calls: a 30-day pilot for 5 to 15 reps, followed by team pricing
around $79 per rep per month plus a $499 per month manager workspace. Enterprise pricing is custom
for larger teams, CRM or call-tool integrations, custom personas, security review, and advanced
admin requirements.

## 6 Objections + Winning Responses

| Objection | Winning response |
| --- | --- |
| "AI cant replace coaching." | "Agreed. This is not trying to replace managers. It gives reps more realistic practice between manager sessions, so live coaching time can focus on sharper moments." |
| "Too expensive." | "That is fair to test. The pilot is meant to prove whether better ramp, more practice, and fewer repeated coaching gaps justify the spend before you roll it out broadly." |
| "My team wont adopt it." | "Adoption has to be designed in. Start with one use case, like cold-call openers or pricing objections, make sessions short, and let managers assign practice tied to this week's pipeline reality." |
| "We already use Gong or call recording." | "Recording tools show what happened after the call. AI Sales Coach gives reps a place to rehearse before the call, especially for moments they are nervous about." |
| "AI buyers will not sound like our real prospects." | "Generic personas are weak. The value is custom buyer prompts built from your ICP, sales cycle, common objections, language, and qualification criteria." |
| "We do not have time to set this up." | "Start narrow: one persona, one call type, one team. If reps can practice a priority conversation in the first week, you will know quickly whether it deserves more rollout." |
